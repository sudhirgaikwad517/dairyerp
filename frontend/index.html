Orders & Dispatch

One order per customer per day. Customers can place, change or cancel their order in the app until the cut-off time (Billing → Dairy Settings); after that it is locked for them. You can enter or change orders here until they are dispatched.

Add / Change an Order

For customers who call the dairy. Works any time before dispatch, even after the customers' cut-off. Quantity 0 cancels that day's order.

Customer Claims

Milk spoiled, short or not delivered, reported by customers with photos. Verify against the delivery photo and signature, then approve or reject. Approved litres are deducted from the bill, or refunded separately.

Customer Profitability

Revenue (net sales, same as the Sales Register) − milk cost (litres delivered × cost price) − transport (each route's fixed cost for the day, shared by litres delivered) = margin. Claimed milk still cost the dairy, so claims lower the margin.

Cost prices: —
What one unit of each product costs the dairy. Used only for profit, never on bills. Delivered orders keep the cost of their day.
